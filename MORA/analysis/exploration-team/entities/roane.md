## Background

- A Senior Colonial Survey officer dispatched from a Crete Line ship to report on the progress of the licensed Loren Two robot colony. He has spent his career in robot-managed colonial environments and has, by his own admission, been "an honest man all my life" until the events of this story. He is dropped off by space-boat at Huyghens' (unexpected) illegal station rather than at the (destroyed) official robot colony.

## Motivation

- **Drives:** Initial: procedural duty, institutional integrity. Post-conversion: empathy (toward Nugget), self-respect (Huyghens' sense), and proportionate policy action.
- **Philosophy:** Moves from deontological rule-following ("I'll send you to prison if I can") to situated utilitarian/virtue ethics ("I won't do it! He's my friend!"). Retains honesty as a value but subordinates it to life-preservation.
- **Future Behavior Prediction:** Will continue to quietly champion mixed-biological colonization within Colonial Survey; will not again reflexively prosecute illegal-but-effective frontier efforts.

## Relationships

- **Bonds:** Post-conversion: strong working relationship with Huyghens; paternal-style bond with Nugget; respectful partnership with the adult bears; grateful regard for the three colony survivors he helped rescue.
- **Betrayal Risks:** None toward his new allies; may still be subject to institutional pressures if his falsification is discovered.
- **Loyalty Forecast:** High to allies, moderate to Colonial Survey (conditional on its alignment with life-preservation).

## Resource and Environmental Adaptation

- **Survival Skills:** Low initially (missed first shot, froze during ambush's opening instant); rises rapidly under mentorship ("the last few seconds of the fight... every bullet hit").
- **Tech Use:** Competent with standard Survey equipment and Huyghens' weapons; skilled at report authoring (the critical final-act skill).

## Long-Term Impact and Legacy Forecast

- His falsified report is the pivot point for the entire novel's outcome. Policy effects ripple forward: the bear-man colonization model gains legal legitimacy; Colonial Survey's robot-default assumption is challenged; 50,000+ future colonists gain a pathway to human (not robot-mediated) frontier life. His career persists intact because his policy recommendation is substantively correct even where his narrative is fabricated.

## Actionable Recommendations

1. In an initial institutional encounter, engage in honest dialogue rather than evasion — Roane's conversion responds to real argument and direct experience.
2. Expose him to ecological reality (let him see bears work, let him form a bond with a cub); policy follows psychology.
3. Trust his post-conversion judgment; he will not arbitrarily reverse himself.
4. Preserve his career — his institutional position is the value-delivering channel for his moral convictions.
