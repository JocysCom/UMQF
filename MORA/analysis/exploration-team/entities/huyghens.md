## Background

- Originates from an unnamed home-planet where mutated Kodiaks (descendants of "Kodius Champion") were bred as combat-capable biological partners. He is officially listed there as a bear-thief, though he confidentially denies it. Travelled to Loren Two as part of a privately funded illegal colonization effort, partnered with the furtive "Kodius Company." His most recent human assistant was killed by a night-walker before the story opens.

## Motivation

- **Drives:** Self-respect; refusal to live as an appendage to robot-managed systems; devotion to his bears; long-term colonization of Loren Two under the bear-man model.
- **Philosophy:** Humans and self-aware biological partners should retain decision-making agency. Robots belong "in cities" and on "airless planets where nothing unexpected ever happens." Survival in novel environments requires beings that can handle the unexpected — humans, bears, and presumably other adaptable minds.
- **Future Behavior Prediction:** Will pursue wider sphex suppression (additional hordes, other breeding grounds), expand the Loren Two colony, and advocate for bear-man frontier policy. Will continue to bend or break regulations when he judges them anti-human.

## Relationships

- **Bonds:** Familial with all four bears; especially with Sitka Pete (command partner) and Faro Nell (protective parallel). Respectful working relationship with Roane post-conversion. Functional-conditioning relationship with Semper the eagle.
- **Betrayal Risks:** Negligible toward allies. High toward institutions he judges to be anti-human.
- **Loyalty Forecast:** Absolute to pack, high to converted allies (Roane, colony survivors), zero to anti-colonization bureaucracy.

## Resource and Environmental Adaptation

- **Survival Skills:** Expert — species-level knowledge of sphexes, plateau topography, night-walker behavior, scent-trail management, antiseptic deodorization.
- **Tech Use:** Pragmatic and unsentimental. Modifies space-phone for short-wave reception; adapts robot-colony flame-casters and soil-sterilizer to human operation; refuses to let tech replace human judgment.

## Long-Term Impact and Legacy Forecast

- Founding figure of legal bear-man colonization on Loren Two. His model will likely be exported to other high-hostility worlds where robot-only approaches fail. Regional sphex population on this plateau will remain suppressed for multiple seasons; other hordes remain as future work. The colony itself, legitimized by Roane's report, will shape Colonial Survey policy toward more flexible mixed-biological settlement.

## Actionable Recommendations

1. Trust him as a combat leader and ecological strategist on Loren Two; do not attempt to impose robot-centric standard procedures.
2. Do not harm, threaten, or attempt to seize the bears — this is his terminal moral line.
3. Engage philosophically and in good faith; he converts honest opponents, not dishonest ones.
4. If Colonial Survey revisits the planet, acknowledge that bear-man symbiosis is the only observed-viable mode and align policy accordingly.
